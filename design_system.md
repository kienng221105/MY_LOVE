## Brand & Style
The design system is built upon a "Dreamy Kawaii" aesthetic, blending modern Korean UI precision with Japanese "kawaii" sensibilities. The brand personality is youthful, innocent, and deeply romantic, designed to evoke feelings of warmth, softness, and playfulness.
